About

I'm a software engineer at WebstaurantStore, working across full-stack features and test automation on a platform that serves millions of customers. I graduated from the University of Utah in December 2025 with a B.S. in Computer Science and an emphasis in Entertainment Arts & Engineering.

Outside of work, I write high-performance C++: a multithreaded ray tracer, a rasterization renderer, and GPU simulations. I've also shipped three games on Steam as a gameplay programmer.

I grew up surrounded by every console and game you could imagine, and my father, a game programmer, gave me my love of games. I believe games can enrich lives, build relationships, and tell stories, and I want to help build the next generation of them.
