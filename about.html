About

Hi, I'm Devin.

Gameplay programmer, graphics enthusiast, and lifelong player.

I'm a student at the University of Utah studying Computer Science with an emphasis in Game Development. I grew up surrounded by every console and game you could imagine, and my father, a game programmer, gave me my love of games.

I believe games can enrich lives, build relationships, and tell stories, and I want to help build the next generation of life-changing experiences. I've worked as both a Gameplay Engineer and a QA Engineer. Lately I've been pulled into graphics programming: ray tracing, real-time rendering, and GPU compute.
